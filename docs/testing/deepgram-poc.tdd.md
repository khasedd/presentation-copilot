## User journeys

1. As downstream transcript logic, receive Deepgram callbacks through unchanged `TranscriptEvent` 1.0 semantics, including revisions, finality, shorter finals, timestamps, failures, and reconnect boundaries.
2. As the experiment operator, stream one explicitly selected microphone without persisting raw audio, transcript/reference text, or credentials, and retain only owner-readable aggregate evidence.
3. As a reviewer, reproduce deterministic behavior without network/audio and distinguish it from the still-required physical spoken trials.

## Coverage and known gaps

`python3 -m trace --count --missing --summary --module unittest discover -s tests` reports **90.0%** line coverage for `transcription.adapters.deepgram` and **84.2%** for `experiments.deepgram_live`. `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v` passes **135/135 tests**.

The deterministic suite mocks transport only where needed; it does not claim microphone acoustics or provider quality. A real in-memory capture, authenticated 1.8-second handshake, 5.6-second disconnect/reconnect preflight, 1.6-second post-fix handshake, 14.55-second content-free smoke, and 14.85-second transition-fix smoke passed. The latest smoke exercised 22 partial callbacks, 20 revisions, two finals, one shorter final, monotonic timestamps, and normal closure. It was not a scripted reading, so its generated accuracy fields are not trial evidence.

Six attempted Trial A reads have not produced a valid scored report. The first three exposed the trailing-window final, mid-word remainder, and provider word-metadata cases. The fourth ran about 77 seconds before the old generic `invalid_provider_message` error; that code cannot distinguish an empty-alternatives callback from optional word-hint variance. The fifth stopped after about 5.5 seconds with `invalid_transition`, exposing the exact-start identity assumption fixed by the shifted-window row above. The sixth reached the harness's old fixed 90-second cutoff while the presenter was still reading. Its generated WER, recall and timing summary are invalid as Trial A evidence and are not used for any acceptance decision. It did account for 89.8 provider-reported billable seconds and an estimated $0.007184, bringing observed completed usage to 128.2 seconds and $0.010256; failed-stream usage remains unavailable and excluded.

The completion regression now requires A/B to observe enough finalized script progress, a provider endpoint, and eight seconds without resumed speech. The duration argument is a 240-second hard safety ceiling, not a success condition; reaching it removes the reserved output instead of scoring the run.

**Valid original Trial A — September 28, 2026:** after the completion fix, the presenter confirmed that the full script was finished before automatic closure. The run recorded 100.65 provider-reported billable seconds. It failed the near-field accuracy thresholds: WER **31.8408%** versus at most 15%, and technical-term recall **36.3636%** (4/11) versus at least 90%. It passed the timing thresholds: interim p95 **79.841 ms** across 87 samples and final p95 **2,021.907 ms** across 24 samples. The stream recorded 117 partial events, 94 partial revisions, 24 finals, 14 shorter finals, four endpoints, monotonic timestamps, no disconnect, and normal closure. Its estimated cost was $0.008052. Observed completed usage at that point totaled 228.85 seconds and an estimated $0.018308; failed-stream usage remained unavailable and excluded. Trials B and C had not run. Exact account balance, promotional-credit origin, expiry, and an independently retrievable `mip_opt_out` request record remained unavailable to the configured key; no billing action was taken.

## Bounded accuracy rerun preparation — September 28, 2026

The original scorer lowercased and extracted lexical `[a-z0-9]+` tokens. An audit pair containing the same spoken content in word and formatted forms scored 100% WER and 0% technical-term recall, demonstrating representation artifacts. The replacement `orthographic_equivalence_v2` method case-folds and removes punctuation/hyphen separators; canonicalizes digit/number-word forms through thousands and spoken decimal forms; equates `kHz` with “kilohertz” and `ms` with “millisecond(s)”; and expands only the enumerated compounds `PipeWire`, `WirePlumber`, `WebSocket`, and `TranscriptEvent` to their separated token forms. It does not perform fuzzy, phonetic, stemming, synonym, or semantic matching, and a regression proves that `three` and `tree` remain different. The historical Trial A result above is unchanged and will not be rescored retroactively.

The fresh request retains all prior privacy/audio/latency settings and adds these ten repeated plain Nova-3 `keyterm` parameters: `Presentation Copilot`, `PipeWire`, `WirePlumber`, `WebSocket`, `Deepgram`, `Nova-3`, `TranscriptEvent`, `Nebius Token Factory`, `NVIDIA Nemotron`, and `Open Parakeet`. No weighted or legacy `keywords` parameter is sent. The next aggregate report also adds S/D/I and reference/hypothesis token counts plus in-memory-only RMS dBFS, peak dBFS, clipping fraction, 50 ms chunk fraction below -50 dBFS, sample/chunk counts, and transmitted/expected-byte continuity. These level metrics can expose silence, clipping, or discontinuity; they do not establish broader noise quality.

The optional short live diagnostic was skipped because official request serialization and deterministic aggregate calculations were covered offline. This preserved the only additional live spend for one fresh full Trial A. Its output used a new ignored file; the original `trial-a.json` remained owner-only historical evidence. Trials B/C and the Parakeet challenger were held pending the bounded rerun and owner review.

**Fresh bounded Trial A rerun — September 28, 2026:** the new aggregate-only `trial-a-keyterm-rerun.json` completed normally and remains ignored with mode 600, separate from the unchanged original. Under `orthographic_equivalence_v2`, WER was **17.0%** with 16 substitutions, 10 deletions, eight insertions, 200 reference tokens, and 198 final-hypothesis tokens. Technical-term recall was **72.7273%** (8/11). Both accuracy metrics therefore still failed the unchanged ≤15% and ≥90% gates. Interim p95 was **140.477 ms** over 102 samples and final p95 was **2,108.261 ms** over 28 samples, both passes. The stream produced 113 partials, 87 revisions, 28 finals, 11 shorter finals, nine endpoints, monotonic timestamps, no disconnect, and normal closure.

Audio transport was continuous: 3,769,600 transmitted bytes equaled expected bytes and 1,884,800 16-bit samples. RMS was **−23.261 dBFS**, peak was **0.0 dBFS**, clipped-sample fraction was **0.001103** (0.1103%), and no 50 ms chunk fell at or below −50 dBFS. This rules out silence and byte/sample discontinuity and shows occasional full-scale overload; these aggregates do not establish how much the clipping or room acoustics affected recognition. The rerun added 117.8 billable seconds and an estimated **$0.009424**, bringing observed completed usage to **346.65 seconds** and an estimated **$0.027732**; failed-stream usage remains unavailable and excluded.

By the predefined decision rule, Deepgram tuning stops because both accuracy gates failed after the single allowed rerun. Trials B/C were not run. The next candidate is the already documented capped NVIDIA/Open Parakeet challenger, but no implementation, Nebius GPU, billing action, or infrastructure launch is authorized before owner review.
